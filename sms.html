UAS SMS Notifications

United All Stars (UAS) offers an optional SMS notification program for UAS members who choose to receive membership-related text messages.

Messages may include membership dues and outstanding balance reminders, bereavement assessment/payment notices, organizational announcements, meetings, events, payment reminders, and other UAS membership-related communications.

How to Opt In

To enroll in the United All Stars (UAS) SMS notification program:

Text START to 202-905-9476

By texting START, you consent to receive recurring SMS messages from United All Stars (UAS) at the mobile number used to send the request.

SMS Program Disclosures

Message frequency varies.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from UAS SMS messages.

Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of membership, purchase, or payment.

Opt-In Confirmation

After texting START, members receive a confirmation message identifying United All Stars (UAS) and confirming enrollment in the UAS SMS program.

The confirmation message states:

United All Stars (UAS): You are now opted in to receive recurring UAS membership SMS messages, including dues, bereavement assessments, payment reminders, events, and organizational updates. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.

Privacy

United All Stars does not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
