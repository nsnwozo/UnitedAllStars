United All Stars SMS Program

United All Stars (UAS) offers an optional SMS notification program for UAS members who choose to receive membership-related text messages.

Messages may include membership dues and outstanding balance reminders, bereavement assessment and payment notices, organizational announcements, meetings, events, payment reminders, and other UAS membership-related communications.

KEYWORD-BASED SMS OPT-IN

This SMS program uses text-message keyword enrollment. There is no web registration form or web consent checkbox.

Using the mobile phone number that you want to enroll in the United All Stars (UAS) SMS program, send the following keyword:

START

to

202-905-9476

The mobile telephone number from which you text START is the number that will be enrolled in the United All Stars (UAS) SMS notification program.

Consent to Receive SMS Messages

By texting START to 202-905-9476 from your mobile telephone, you expressly consent to receive recurring SMS messages from United All Stars (UAS) at the mobile telephone number from which you sent the START request.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of membership, purchase, or payment.

Message frequency varies.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Reply HELP for assistance.

What Messages Will I Receive?

Members who opt in may receive SMS communications from United All Stars (UAS) concerning:

  • Membership dues
  • Outstanding membership balances
  • Bereavement assessments
  • Bereavement payment reminders
  • Other payment reminders
  • UAS meetings
  • UAS events
  • Organizational announcements
  • Other UAS membership-related information

What Happens After I Text START?

After a member texts START to 202-905-9476, United All Stars (UAS) sends an automated confirmation message confirming enrollment in the UAS SMS notification program.

The confirmation message is:

United All Stars (UAS): You are now opted in to receive recurring UAS membership SMS messages, including dues, bereavement assessments, payment reminders, events, and organizational updates. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.

How to Opt Out

You may cancel your participation in the United All Stars (UAS) SMS program at any time by replying STOP.

After receiving your STOP request, you will no longer receive recurring UAS SMS communications unless you subsequently opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance with the United All Stars (UAS) SMS program.

You may also contact United All Stars at: unitedallstarsmd@gmail.com

Privacy

United All Stars (UAS) does not sell or share SMS opt-in data, mobile telephone numbers, or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

For additional information about how United All Stars handles member information, please review our Privacy Policy.
