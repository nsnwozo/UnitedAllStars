About United All Stars

United All Stars (UAS) is a membership-based community organization dedicated to fellowship, community engagement, member support, and organizational activities.

UAS communicates with its members regarding membership dues, outstanding balances, bereavement assessments and payments, meetings, events, organizational announcements, and other membership-related matters.

UAS SMS Communications

United All Stars provides an optional SMS notification program for members who choose to receive membership-related text messages.

Members may receive notifications regarding dues, outstanding balances, bereavement assessments, payment reminders, meetings, events, organizational announcements, and other UAS membership information.
