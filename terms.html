UAS SMS Terms

United All Stars (UAS) provides an optional SMS notification program for members who choose to receive UAS membership-related communications.

Messages may include membership dues and outstanding balance reminders, bereavement assessment/payment notices, organizational announcements, meetings, events, payment reminders, and other membership-related communications.

Opt-In

Members may opt in by texting START to 202-905-9476.

By texting START, you consent to receive recurring SMS messages from United All Stars (UAS) at the mobile number used to opt in.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies depending on UAS membership activities and communications.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from UAS SMS messages. After opting out, you will no longer receive recurring UAS SMS communications unless you subsequently opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact United All Stars at unitedallstarsmd@gmail.com.

Consent

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of membership, purchase, or payment.

Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to your wireless carrier's network availability.

Privacy

Your participation in the UAS SMS program is also governed by our Privacy Policy.
