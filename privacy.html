Information We Collect

United All Stars (UAS) may collect information voluntarily provided by members, including names, mobile telephone numbers, email addresses, membership information, payment-related information, and SMS opt-in and consent information.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to administer UAS membership and communicate with members regarding membership dues, outstanding balances, bereavement assessments/payments, organizational announcements, meetings, events, payment reminders, and other membership-related matters.

SMS Privacy

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and SMS consent information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be disclosed when required by law or to service providers acting on behalf of UAS when necessary to provide organizational services, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

SMS Communications

Members who opt in may receive recurring UAS membership-related SMS messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

SMS Consent

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of membership, purchase, or payment.
